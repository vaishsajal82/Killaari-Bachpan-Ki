About Kilkaari

Why Kilkaari Exists

Two gaps we set out to close

Kilkaari is an initiative to secure early education for children from economically disadvantaged and marginalized backgrounds, and to bring them into mainstream schooling — while building a community of people who believe education is the most reliable way to lift society up.

The First Gap

Children in this group are often left out of any form of education — school, home, or social — and society largely stays indifferent to it. Kilkaari fills that gap, free of charge.

The Second Gap

India's school system is age-based, not knowledge-based. We bring children up to the right level first, then help enrol them in the class they're actually ready for.

Our Approach

Mental and moral strength alongside academics — sports, ethics and civic pride treated as seriously as the syllabus, with parents kept engaged throughout.

Our Purpose (उद्देश्य)

What we're working toward

01

Educate & Build Character

Provide education to children from economically weaker sections, building them mentally and morally so they can live self-reliant, dignified lives.

02

A Lifelong Love of Learning

Spark curiosity that outlasts the program itself — with sports, moral education and civic pride valued alongside academics.

03

Build a Community

Bring together people who see education as the best tool for social change, and are willing to give time and resources to it.

04

A Movement, Not Just a Program

Turn Kilkaari's spirit into an education movement — doing the most with limited resources, powered by community support.
