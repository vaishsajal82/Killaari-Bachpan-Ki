About Kilkaari

Connecting disadvantaged children back to mainstream education

Kilkaari is an initiative to secure early education for children from economically disadvantaged and marginalized backgrounds, and to bring them into mainstream schooling. Alongside this, we bring together people who believe education is the most reliable way to lift society up. We supply the basics — copybooks, pencils, pens — and age-appropriate lessons, and we stay in touch with parents so their support for a child's schooling doesn't fade.

Why This Is Needed

Children in this group are often left out of any form of education — school, home, or social — and society largely stays indifferent to it. Kilkaari fills that gap, free of charge.

Age vs. Knowledge

India's school system is age-based, not knowledge-based. We bring children up to the right level first, then help enrol them in the class they're actually ready for.

Our Values

Mental and moral strength alongside academics — with sports, ethics and a sense of civic pride treated as seriously as the syllabus.
